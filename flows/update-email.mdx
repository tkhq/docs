You can update the email address on your Turnkey user in the [Turnkey dashboard](https://app.turnkey.com). You might want to do this if:

- You are moving to a new work email, for example after a company rename or domain change.
- You want notifications and email authentication codes delivered to a different inbox.
- The address was entered incorrectly when the user was created.
- You want to switch from a personal address to a shared or role-based one, such as `ops@` or `security@`.

Changing the email address requires approval from one of your authenticators, so keep your passkey or security key handy.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your account menu">
    Select your name in the top right corner of the dashboard, then choose **My Profile**.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/dashboard/update-email/01-account-menu.png"
        alt="Open your account menu"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the editor">
    On your profile, select the **Edit user** button.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/dashboard/update-email/02-profile.png"
        alt="Open the editor"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Change the email address">
    Replace the email address with the new one and select **Continue**.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/dashboard/update-email/03-edit-dialog.png"
        alt="Change the email address"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Approve the change">
    Confirm the update with your authenticator. Choose **This Device** for a passkey or **Security Key** for a hardware key.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/dashboard/update-email/04-confirm.png"
        alt="Approve the change"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Email updated">
    Your profile now shows the new email address.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/dashboard/update-email/05-saved.png"
        alt="Email updated"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
