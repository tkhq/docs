You can invite someone to join your organization from the **Team** page of the
[Turnkey dashboard](https://app.turnkey.com). The invitation creates a team member in your
parent organization, which is the organization you sign in to. It does not apply to
sub-organizations. To add users to a sub-organization, use the
[create users activity](/api-reference/activities/create-users). If you need a user for
programmatic API access rather than a person, choose **Service user** instead. You might want
to invite a team member if:

- A new engineer, operator, or reviewer needs their own dashboard login instead of sharing one.
- You want a second person in the [root quorum](/features/users/root-quorum) so that losing a
  single passkey cannot lock you out of the organization.
- You are setting up approval workflows and need more users to act as approvers in
  [policies](/features/policies/overview).
- Someone on your team needs to review activity, wallets, or billing in the dashboard.

Before you start, keep the following in mind:

- The invitee receives an email with a link to join. The link is valid for 48 hours. If it
  expires, delete the pending invitation and send a new one.
- When they accept, they register a passkey or security key, which becomes their first
  authenticator. Until then there is no user in the organization for them.
- A new team member has no permissions until a policy grants them or they join the root
  quorum. Any user tags you pick during the invitation are assigned when they accept, so
  policies that reference those tags apply right away.
- An organization can hold up to 100 users and 100 pending invitations. See
  [resource limits](/reference/resource-limits).

<Warning>
  Anyone who opens the invitation link can join your organization as that team member, so
  double-check the email address before you approve the invitation.
</Warning>

Inviting a team member and deleting an invitation each require approval from one of your
authenticators, so keep your passkey or security key handy.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Team page">
    In the left navigation, select **Team**, then select **New User** in the top right.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/dashboard/invite-user/01-team-page.png"
        alt="Open the Team page"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the user type">
    Choose **Team member**.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/dashboard/invite-user/02-new-user-menu.png"
        alt="Choose the user type"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the team member's details">
    Enter the team member's full name and email address. If your organization has user tags, you can also pick the ones they should start with. Select **Continue**.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/dashboard/invite-user/03-invite-form.png"
        alt="Enter the team member's details"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Approve the invitation">
    Confirm with your authenticator. Choose **This Device** for a passkey or **Security Key** for a hardware key.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/dashboard/invite-user/04-approve-invite.png"
        alt="Approve the invitation"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Invitation sent">
    The new member appears in the team list with the status **Invited** and receives an email with a link to join. To manage a pending invitation, select its row.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/dashboard/invite-user/05-invited.png"
        alt="Invitation sent"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the pending invitation">
    The invitation's details show who was invited and when. To cancel it, select the **Delete invitation** icon.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/dashboard/invite-user/06-invited-user.png"
        alt="Open the pending invitation"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm the deletion">
    Select **Delete**, then approve with your authenticator as before.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/dashboard/invite-user/07-confirm-delete.png"
        alt="Confirm the deletion"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Invitation deleted">
    You return to the team list, and the pending invitation is gone. The link in the invitation email no longer works.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/dashboard/invite-user/08-deleted.png"
        alt="Invitation deleted"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
