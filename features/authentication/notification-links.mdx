## What it is

Notification links is a parent-organization setting that lists the hostnames allowed in authentication and OTP emails and SMS. Open it in the [Turnkey Dashboard](https://app.turnkey.com/authentication/domain-allowlist) at **Dashboard → Authentication → Notification links**.

Domains are bare hostnames such as `example.com`, without a scheme, path, port, or wildcard. Allowlist every hostname that appears in an email, OTP, or SMS, including hosts used only for images. An empty list blocks notifications that contain links.

## What it does

A saved domain covers that host, every subdomain, and every path. It applies to email magic links, email recovery, and email or SMS OTP messages sent for that parent organization and its sub-organizations.

An empty list rejects any link in those messages. App schemes and IP addresses are blocked. A `mailto:` address is not a link.

`example.com` allows these:

- `https://example.com/login`
- `https://app.example.com/reset`
- `https://cdn.example.com/logo.png`
