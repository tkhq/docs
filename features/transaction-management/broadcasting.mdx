Broadcasting lets a Turnkey wallet send a transaction with a single activity. You pass a minimal payload; Turnkey constructs the transaction, signs it in a secure enclave, broadcasts it, and tracks it until it lands. Turnkey can also pay the network fee for you with [gas sponsorship](/features/transaction-management/broadcasting/gas-sponsorship). You do not need your own RPC provider.

## How it works

1. **Send.** Call `ethSendTransaction` or `solSendTransaction` with the sending address, the chain's `caip2`, the transaction, and `sponsor`. Your policies are evaluated like any other activity. See [Send a transaction](/features/transaction-management/broadcasting/send-transaction).
2. **Turnkey builds, signs, and broadcasts.** Turnkey fills in what you omit (on EVM, the nonce and fees; on Solana, a recent blockhash, compute unit limit, and priority fee), signs, and broadcasts.
3. **Track the result.** You get a `sendTransactionStatusId`. Poll it or subscribe to webhooks until the transaction is `INCLUDED` or `FAILED`. See [Track transaction status](/features/transaction-management/broadcasting/track-status).

The same call sends sponsored and non-sponsored transactions; `sponsor` only controls who pays the fee. Broadcasting is available on Pay-as-you-go, Pro, and Enterprise plans, for both.

## What you can send

- **EVM:** any transaction, not just transfers: contract calls, contract deployments, or any valid EVM operation. Several calls can be sent as one atomic transaction with [batching](/features/transaction-management/broadcasting/send-transaction#batch-evm-calls).
- **Solana:** any transaction you build, signed by one or more Turnkey signers. See [Solana transactions](/features/networks/solana-transactions). On Solana, fee sponsorship does not cover the rent for accounts a transaction creates; that is [rent sponsorship](/features/networks/solana-rent-refunds), a separate Enterprise feature.

See [Supported chains](/features/transaction-management#supported-chains) for the networks.

## How Turnkey gets a transaction included

- **Simulation.** When Turnkey estimates a transaction's fees, it also checks whether the transaction would fail, and rejects it before it is sent, with the reason. See [Track transaction status](/features/transaction-management/broadcasting/track-status#evm-smart-contract-transaction-errors).
- **Stuck sponsored transactions.** Turnkey keeps a sponsored transaction moving until it lands or can no longer land, using whatever the chain allows, such as resubmitting it or replacing it with a higher fee.
- **Stuck non-sponsored transactions.** A replacement needs a new signature from your wallet, so Turnkey does not replace these on its own. Send the transaction again yourself, for example with the same nonce and a higher fee on EVM.
- **Status.** A transaction can stay `BROADCASTING` for a while and still land. Poll [`get_send_transaction_status`](/api-reference/queries/get-send-transaction-status) or subscribe to the `SEND_TRANSACTION_STATUS_UPDATES` [webhook](/features/webhooks/overview) for the final status.

## Policies

You write policies against sent transactions with the `eth.tx` namespace on EVM and `solana.tx` on Solana. They apply the same way whether or not the transaction is sponsored. See [Policy language](/features/policies/language).
