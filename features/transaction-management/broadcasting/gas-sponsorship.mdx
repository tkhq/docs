---
title: "Gas sponsorship and spend limits"
description: "Pay network fees for your users' transactions, cap sponsored spend per organization and sub-organization, and monitor usage."
---

With gas sponsorship, Turnkey pays the network fee for a transaction, so the sending wallet never needs to hold the chain's native token. Set `sponsor: true` on a send to sponsor it, or `sponsor: false` to have the wallet pay. Either way, Turnkey constructs, signs, broadcasts, and tracks the transaction; the flag only controls who pays the fee.

You can also integrate your own gas service where your transaction flow supports it. See [AA wallets](/features/wallets/aa-wallets) for examples of third-party sponsorship integrations.

## Enable sponsorship

Enable Gas Sponsorship for your organization in the Turnkey dashboard, then send with `sponsor: true` and the `caip2` of a [supported chain](/features/transaction-management#supported-chains).

<Note>
  Gas Sponsorship is available on **Pay-as-you-go**, **Pro**, and **Enterprise** plans.

  - **Pay-as-you-go:** Up to $10 per month
  - **Pro:** Up to $25 per month
  - **Enterprise:** Unlimited spend, with configurable time windows

  Limits apply over a rolling window of up to 30 days. A shorter window gets a proportional share of the monthly limit. Solana rent sponsorship is available on Enterprise plans. If you need higher limits, or rent sponsorship on a Pay-as-you-go or Pro plan, please [reach out](https://www.turnkey.com/contact-us)!
</Note>

On Solana, fee sponsorship and rent sponsorship are separate. `Sponsor Solana Rent` is disabled by default and must be enabled in the dashboard before Turnkey pre-funds rent for account creation. See [Solana Rent Sponsorship](/features/networks/solana-rent-refunds).

## Spend limits

Turnkey gives you USD-denominated controls over gas sponsorship spend at two levels:

- **Organization-wide limit:** the cap on total sponsored spend across your parent organization and all of its sub-organizations.
- **Sub-organization limit:** the cap that applies to each of your sub-organizations.

You set limit values and time intervals in the dashboard.

The time interval makes each limit a spending rate rather than a lifetime total. Limits apply over a rolling window, so sponsored spend frees up as older transactions age out, and normal usage keeps being sponsored while a runaway integration, an abusive end user, or a spike in network fees is capped.

<Tip>
  Turnkey provides fee sponsorship and transaction broadcasting services only. In high-fee or congested network conditions, delays or non-inclusion may occur. It is the developer's responsibility to ensure appropriate spend limits are in place.
</Tip>

## Check gas usage

Query current usage against the active limit with [`get_gas_usage`](/api-reference/queries/get-gas-usage). We recommend checking a sub-organization's usage on the client side, so your application can handle a user approaching or exceeding the limit, and monitoring your organization-wide usage regularly.

```ts
const resp = await httpClient?.getGasUsage({});
if (resp && Number(resp.usageUsd) > Number(resp.windowLimitUsd)) {
  console.error("Gas usage limit exceeded for sponsored transactions");
}
```

## Billing

Turnkey passes transaction fee costs through to you as a line item at the end of the month. You pay based on the USD value of fees at time of broadcast; Turnkey internalizes the inventory risk of token price changes.

## Sponsorship contracts and security

On EVM chains, sponsorship runs through Turnkey's own gas station contracts, which are open source on [GitHub](https://github.com/tkhq/gas-station) and optimized to reduce the gas overhead of each sponsored transaction.

Some gas sponsorship setups are subject to replay attacks: if the provider's infrastructure is compromised, one sponsorship request can be replayed with different nonces to create several transactions. Turnkey constructs transactions in enclaves, and as long as the request includes the relevant nonce or blockhash, only one transaction can be created from it. The user's authenticator signs the request and the enclave verifies the signature, so a malicious actor cannot modify or replay it. Our SDKs include a gas station nonce in sponsored requests by default.

Policies apply the same way to sponsored and non-sponsored transactions, so you can switch between them without changing your policies. Turnkey sets all fee-related fields to 0 for sponsored transactions.
