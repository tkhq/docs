---
title: "OTP auth (deprecated)"
description: "Migration guidance for this deprecated authentication flow."
---

<Warning>
  This authentication flow is deprecated. Do not use it for new integrations.
</Warning>

Use encrypted OTP verification and client-bound sessions: `INIT_OTP_V3` → `VERIFY_OTP_V2` → `OTP_LOGIN_V2`.

Follow the [replacement flow and migration guidance](/features/authentication/otp-migration-guide) before updating your integration. The migration covers client-side encryption, client signing, and policy updates; plan them as one change.
